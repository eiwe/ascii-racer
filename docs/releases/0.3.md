Higher stakes: a faster car with a real limit, nitro, sound and music, a pause menu, and five lighting modes.

## New in 0.3

- **Faster, with a limit.** Top speed is up from 7.7 to 9.8 and cornering grip is lower relative to it, so the circuit can no longer be driven flat. The S-bend and the wide sweeper are still flat; the gentler bends need a lift; the 90° corners and the hairpin need braking. Overdrive a corner and the car slides, a countersteer can catch it, and ignoring it spins you. The counter-steer assist is weaker than before.
- **Nitro.** Hold Left Shift or click the left stick. A full tank is 1.5 seconds and pushes the car to about 12.7. It refills by drifting, or slowly by itself. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows the tank.
- **Sound.** A recorded petrol engine with a simulated five-speed gearbox, tyre squeal at the grip limit, a skid in the drift, landing thumps, kerb knocks, impacts and nitro.
- **Music.** A title track under the demo reel and a race track while driving, crossfaded.
- **Pause menu.** `Esc` or Start pauses and opens a menu: resume, restart, master, music and effects volume, a sound check that plays each sound on its own, return to the title, quit. Volumes are saved between runs. `M` mutes.
- **Lighting modes** on keys `1`–`5`: classic (unchanged and still the default), low sun, hatched shadows, glint, and a moving sun. In modes 2–5, `J`/`L` swing the sun and `I`/`K` raise and lower it.
- **One glyph method.** The renderer now always uses shape matching; the ramp, edges, overstrike and hatch methods were removed.
- The jump was moved and reshaped for the higher speed.

## First launch on macOS

The macOS build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time. Either:

- Open the app, dismiss the warning, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**; or
- after unzipping, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/"ASCII Racer.app"` in Terminal (adjust the path).

## Known limitations

- The handling, the nitro and the audio mix were tuned against scripted tests and recordings, with limited hands-on driving and listening so far. Expect them to change.
- Some sounds were cut from lossy preview files, and there is no engine recording above about 2,600 rpm, so the top of the rev range is that loop pitched up. See `assets/audio/CREDITS.md`.
- The `heavy` preset bottoms out on the jump landing.
- A `custom` handling preset saved in an earlier version keeps its old, slower values.
- The Retina defaults on macOS are untested, and the Windows build has only been run under Wine, not on a Windows PC.
- One track, one car, no opponents.
