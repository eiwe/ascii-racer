Three circuits and a level select: a dirt arena with banked turns and jumps, a mountain road with snow, a time of day for each, plus a smooth camera switch and calmer keyboard steering at speed.

**Play in the browser:** https://eiwe.github.io/ascii-racer/

## New in 0.5

- **Level select.** After the demo reel you choose the circuit on a large live preview, with arrows to the next one. For each circuit you pick the time of day (morning, noon or night) and the opponent (off, or 1 to 5). Each circuit remembers its own choices and best lap. The pause menu has a Change Track entry.
- **Dust Bowl.** A 169 m dirt arena: two steep banked turns with a tyre wall round the outside, a table-top, a double, a step-down and a tight flat corner. On dirt the tyres let go earlier and more gently, slides hold their speed, and braking takes about a fifth longer. Sliding wheels throw up dust, and the tyres sound like gravel.
- **Frost Pass.** A 171 m mountain road on asphalt: two climbing switchbacks, a crest, a long downhill sweeper and a chicane. About a third of the lap is snow: the braking zone into the first switchback, one whole corner, the inside two thirds of the sweeper (the short way on snow or the long way on asphalt) and strips along the edge. Snow has far less grip than dirt and braking takes about half as long again.
- **Morning and noon.** Two new lighting looks, on keys `8` and `9` and as the level select's times of day. Night is the existing headlight mode.
- **Smooth camera switch.** The camera control now glides between the isometric and chase views in about half a second instead of cutting.
- **Calmer keyboard steering at speed.** Steering keys now turn the wheels in more slowly the faster you go: full lock takes 0.36 s flat out (it was 0.17 s at every speed), so a tap at speed is a small correction. Nothing changes at low speed, and letting go, changing direction and counter-steering into a slide are as quick as before. The gamepad stick is unchanged. The tuning panel has a new `key rate fast` value for it.
- **The opponent races on all three circuits.** Every level laps each circuit cleanly; the lower levels roll over some of Dust Bowl's jumps instead of clearing them.

## Changed

- **Glyph size follows the display.** The default size is now worked out the same way on every screen, so the picture stays visibly made of characters. Two things set it: the display's scaling (Retina, Windows scaling, a Wayland output scale, the browser's device pixel ratio), and a limit on how many rows of text fit the screen's height, which catches dense screens that report no scaling, such as a 4K monitor at 100 %. In the browser a Retina display now starts at 22 px (it was 16). A 4K monitor at 100 % starts at 14 px on the desktop and 18 px in the browser instead of 9 and 12. Ordinary 1080p and 1440p screens are unchanged, and a size you set yourself with `-` / `=` still wins.
- **Glyph measure in the debug HUD.** The HUD (`H`) shows the glyph size in pixels, its logical size (pixels divided by the display scaling) and the rows per screen height, so two screens can be compared.
- **HUD text follows Windows and Wayland scaling**, as it already did on macOS and in the browser.
- **Dust Bowl and Frost Pass ship with the web build**; the browser saves the selected circuit and its settings like the other options.

## Changed controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Level select: circuit, or the value of the selected row | Left / right, or `A` / `D` | D-pad or left stick |
| Level select: move between preview, time of day, opponent and Start | Up / down, or `W` / `S` | D-pad or left stick |
| Start the race | `Enter` or `Space` | A or Start |
| Back to the demo reel | `Esc` | B |
| Lighting mode | `1`–`9` (new: `8` morning, `9` noon) | |

Any key on the demo reel now opens the level select, with the circuit you last drove already selected. Everything else is in the [README](https://github.com/eiwe/ascii-racer#readme).

The desktop builds need a GPU with Vulkan (Windows, Linux), Direct3D 12 (Windows) or Metal (macOS). The web build needs a desktop browser with WebGL 2.

## First launch on macOS

The macOS build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time. Either:

- Open the app, dismiss the warning, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**; or
- after unzipping, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/"ASCII Racer.app"` in Terminal (adjust the path).

## Known limitations

- The web build draws the car without its glint in the sunset, glint, morning and noon looks. Otherwise it looks close to the desktop builds but not identical; night differs most. Rumble in the browser is untested.
- The dirt and snow tyre sounds are level-matched by measurement and made from recordings of a bike on gravel and footsteps in snow, not of car tyres.
- On Dust Bowl, a crooked take-off from the step-down on nitro can leave the car off the road for a few seconds.
- The opponent is a rival on the road, not yet a race: there is a start, but no lap count, finish or results. Its level steps on Frost Pass are uneven.
- Night is experimental: the far side of a corner outside the headlight beam is sparse.
- A lighting mode picked with the number keys, the camera and the handling preset (other than a saved `custom`) are not kept between runs.
- The `heavy` preset bottoms out on Figure Eight's jump landing.
- The Retina defaults on macOS and the scaling on Windows are untested on real hardware; the Windows build has only been run under Wine.
