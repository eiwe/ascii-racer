The second Android preview replaces tilt steering as the default with touch steering, and lets you switch between four control schemes on the phone. The first preview's tilt steering lagged and tilted the whole picture with the phone.

**The new controls were built and checked on a desktop that simulates the phone's touches; they have not yet been run on a real phone.**

Only the Android build is attached. Windows, Linux, macOS and the browser are unchanged from [0.5](https://github.com/eiwe/ascii-racer/releases/tag/v0.5). This build installs over 0.6-android.1.

## Controls

Pause (**||** top right, or the back button) and change **CONTROLS** to switch scheme. The choice is saved. The throttle is automatic in all of them once the start lights go out.

| CONTROLS | Steering | Brake and nitro |
|---|---|---|
| **BUTTONS** (default) | Hold **<** or **>** bottom left. It steers like the keyboard: gently at speed, quickly when slow. Slide the thumb across to change direction. | **BRAKE** and **NITRO** bottom right |
| **SLIDER** | Put the left thumb down anywhere on the left half and slide sideways. The further you slide, the more it steers; lift to go straight. | **BRAKE** and **NITRO** bottom right |
| **SPLIT** | Hold the left half of the screen to steer left, the right half to steer right. | Both halves together brake. Small **NITRO** bottom centre. |
| **TILT** | Tilt the phone like a steering wheel, as in the first preview. | **BRAKE** and **NITRO** bottom right |

BRAKE held at a standstill reverses. In the menus: tap anywhere on the demo reel for the level select, tap the arrows or swipe sideways to change circuit, tap a row to change it, tap START. A connected keyboard or gamepad works as on the desktop.

## Also new

- **Frame rate in the pause menu.** The bottom line of the pause menu shows the average frame rate over the last 5 seconds of racing and the slowest single frame, for example `58 fps (low 41)`. If the game feels laggy, this number says whether the phone is the cause.
- **Level display, with TILT only.** The picture turns against the phone's roll, up to 30 degrees, so the horizon stays level while you tilt. **LEVEL DISPLAY** and **FLIP STEERING** appear in the pause menu when CONTROLS is TILT. It draws about twice as many characters, so turn it off first if tilt runs slowly.
- **Pause menu rows are easier to hit.** Each row's tap area covers its full height.

## Install

Download `ascii-racer-0.6-android.2-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from the browser or file manager you opened it with. It needs a 64-bit ARM phone with OpenGL ES 3.

## What to expect

- Button sizes and positions were chosen on a desktop window, not under real thumbs.
- Several fingers at once were tested with simulated touches only.
- Unverified on a device: the notch and rounded-corner margins, pausing when the app goes to the background, and the level display.
- There is no handbrake button, so handbrake drifts need a gamepad.
