The third Android preview makes the phone controls easier to drive: steering stops at the tyres' grip limit, two driving aids help with corners, and the thumb slider is now the default with twice the travel. The second preview's controls were too sensitive in every scheme.

**These changes were measured on a desktop that simulates the phone's touches and tilt; they have not yet been run on a real phone.** Every starting value is a guess and can be changed in the pause menu while racing.

Only the Android build is attached. Windows, Linux, macOS and the browser are unchanged from [0.5](https://github.com/eiwe/ascii-racer/releases/tag/v0.5). This build installs over the earlier previews. If you already chose a control scheme in an earlier preview, that choice is kept; change **CONTROLS** to SLIDER to try the new default.

## What changed

- **Steering stops at the grip limit.** Full thumb travel, full tilt or a held button now asks for slightly more wheel than the tyres can use at the current speed and on the surface under the car, instead of about twice that. At top speed the whole range is usable. At low speed nothing changes.
- **STEER ASSIST** (50 % by default). The road's curve turns the wheels and your steering adds on top. It keeps the line you last steered to and never pulls to the centre. It fades out off the road, in the air, in a drift, in reverse and when the car faces the wrong way.
- **BRAKE ASSIST** (on by default). The automatic throttle lifts before corners. It never brakes; the BRAKE button works as before.
- **SLIDER is the default**, with twice the thumb travel (about a third of the screen height to full steering).
- **SENSITIVITY** for the slider and for tilt, saved separately.

## Tuning on the phone

Pause with **||** (top right) or the back button. The menu now has two pages; **MORE SETTINGS** opens the second and **BACK** returns. Changes apply as soon as you resume.

| Row | Default | Range | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| CONTROLS | SLIDER | SLIDER, BUTTONS, SPLIT, TILT | 1 |
| SENSITIVITY (slider and tilt) | 1.0 | 0.5 to 2.0. Higher steers more for the same movement; 2.0 on the slider is the second preview's travel. | 1 |
| STEER ASSIST | 50 % | OFF, 25 % to 100 %. At 100 % with BRAKE ASSIST on the car laps every circuit hands off. | 1 |
| BRAKE ASSIST | ON | ON / OFF | 1 |
| GRIP MARGIN | 1.15 | OFF, 1.00 to 1.60. Full steering is the grip limit times this; OFF gives the old steering. | 2 |
| CORNER SPEED | 100 % | 80 % to 120 %. Higher makes the brake assist lift later. | 2 |

With GRIP MARGIN off, STEER ASSIST off, BRAKE ASSIST off and slider SENSITIVITY 2.0, the game drives exactly as the second preview did.

The aids apply to touch and tilt steering only. A connected keyboard or gamepad steers as on the desktop.

## Install

Download `ascii-racer-0.6-android.3-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from the browser or file manager you opened it with. It needs a 64-bit ARM phone with OpenGL ES 3.

## What to expect

- How the aids feel under a real thumb is unknown, including whether 50 % assist leaves you in charge of the line.
- In the simulation, a driver who reacts 100 ms late and steers hard still leaves the road. The same driver steering gently with the assist on stays on it.
- On Frost Pass, with snow under the outside wheels only, full steering reaches about 78 % of the best turn rate at top speed. Raising GRIP MARGIN gives more.
- TILT still answers more slowly than the slider (about 0.14 s against 0.03 s), because of the smoothing on the tilt sensor.
- The pause menu's bottom line still shows the frame rate of the last 5 seconds of racing, for example `58 fps (low 41)`.
- There is no handbrake button, so handbrake drifts need a gamepad.
